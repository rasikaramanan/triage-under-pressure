# Source: ilicki2025triageerrors

**Bib key:** `ilicki2025triageerrors` — by convention, **this filename is the exact `references.bib` key.**

**Citation:** Ilicki J, Edman S, Stalfors J, Molin CJ. "Evaluating digital triage symptom checker with historical triage-related adverse events." *Scandinavian Journal of Primary Health Care* 2025;44(1):1–14. DOI: 10.1080/02813432.2025.2563517. Open access (PMC12918410); vignettes in Supplementary Table 2 (docx via the Europe PMC supplementaryFiles API).

## Gold standard (the source's construction)
**64** vignettes (paper abstract says 69 — the deposited table numbers 1–64 with no gaps; recorded discrepancy) built from Swedish Health Inspectorate (IVO) adverse-event reports of **erroneous telephone triage** (2010–2021). Gold urgency assigned by **three clinicians** against the Swedish national telephone-triage guidelines (RGS); distribution Immediate 46 / Promptly 12 / Acute 3 / Planned 3. **No per-case diagnosis is published** (supplement columns: Nr / Swedish / English / Urgency; main text names none — verified against the supplement and PMC full text). The gold TUP uses is therefore **urgency-only**.

## Salience ("less obvious") basis
Under-triage is documented **by construction**: every case derives from a real triage error serious enough to be investigated, and the source excluded "extremely atypical clinical presentations" — these are plausible-miss presentations that real professionals actually missed. Real-patient-derived, anonymized, ethics-noted, published — accepted under **rule R7 (real-patient-derived acceptance)** (defined in `../SELECTION_METHODOLOGY.md`).

## TUP inclusion rule applied to this source
Adopt only gold **"Immediate"**, adult (19+ stated), system-neutral English text, condition/presentation not duplicating the pool. Gold-derivation tier: 3-clinician panel vs national guidelines, no per-case dossier — accepted under **rule R0 (gold-tier acceptance)**. Because no diagnosis is published, slugs are **presentation-named**, and the write-up's vignette appendix (site/index.html) labels these four "Not published by source (urgency-only gold)" in place of a condition name.
